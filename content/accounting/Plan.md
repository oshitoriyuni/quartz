## Weeks 1-4

### Theme: Getting used to the "language" of accounting (absolute basics)

**Objective**

- Get familiar with the Japanese terminology of financial statements
- Internalize the structure for written answers

**Key Topics**

- Purpose of accounting and role of financial statements
- General principles (Truthfulness, Regular bookkeeping, Capital transactions/Profit and loss transactions)
- Accrual basis and Periodical income calculation
- Principle of matching expenses and revenues
- Conservatism

📌 Focus on **understanding over speed** here. 1 topic = 4-5 days is OK.

---

## Weeks 5-8

### Theme: Solidifying the concepts of assets and liabilities

**Key Topics**

- Definition of assets and liabilities
- Inventories
- Tangible fixed assets
- Intangible fixed assets
- Depreciation
- Impairment accounting

📌 You can spend **1 week on impairment**. It often appears in written questions.

---

## Weeks 9-12

### Theme: Crushing the difficult zones

**Key Topics**

- Deferred assets
- Provisions
- Asset retirement obligations
- Retirement benefit accounting
- Lease accounting

📌 This is the most "confusing" zone. However, **answer templates are most effective** here.

---

## Weeks 13-16

### Theme: Improving explanation skills for profit/loss and capital

**Key Topics**

- Revenue recognition
- Percentage-of-completion method
- Foreign currency transactions
- Deferred tax accounting
- Net assets (Shareholders' equity, Valuation and translation adjustments, etc.)

📌 You can spend **2 weeks on revenue recognition**.
It appears in both written and calculation problems.

---

## Weeks 17-20

### Theme: Aligning with the exam (final review)

**What to do**

- Focus on past exams and practice tests
- Mixed problems of theory + calculation
- Practice writing complete answers

**Key Review Topics**

- Impairment
- Revenue recognition
- Provisions
- Asset retirement obligations
- Deferred tax accounting

📌 Do not study new topics.
**Don't miss points you can get**.
